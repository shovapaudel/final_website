I am Shova Paudel, a forestry graduate with honors in 2025. Some of my hobbies include reading books, painting, travelling, and exploring new places.

My growing passion for academic research has motivated me to dedicate myself in research and to expand my knowledge in the field. My research interests include habitat suitability modeling using various models, GIS & Remote Sensing, biodiversity conservation, and rangeland ecosystems. Overall, I am interested in learning and developing new skills.

My undergraduate research focused specifically on modeling the current and future habitat suitability of Parthenium hysterophorus in Nepal under different climate scenarios. This helped me gain experience in spatial data analysis, modeling, GIS, and statistical approaches.
